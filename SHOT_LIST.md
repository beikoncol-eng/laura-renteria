# Shot List — "Working Lifestyle" Session

**Purpose:** document Laura *at work*, not more portraits. The current library is
one styled studio session — strong but one-note. This session completes the
storytelling: it supplies the documentary, detail, and environment frames the
editorial system needs, and it feeds the **three visual worlds** (Creative
Direction · Digital Marketing · Image Consulting).

## Direction (applies to every frame)
- **Documentary, not posed.** Laura should look *interrupted while working*, never
  performing for the camera. The camera observes; it does not direct.
- **Natural light** where possible; soft, cloudy-daylight quality. No hard flash,
  no colored gels, no cinematic contrast.
- **Palette continuity** with the existing work: warm white, cream, stone,
  charcoal-brown, muted navy, chrome. Neutral wardrobe, timeless tailoring.
- **Alternate scale deliberately:** wide (environment) → medium (interaction) →
  close (hands/face) → detail (objects). Every section needs a different scale.
- **Capture verticals (3:4 / 4:5) AND a few 16:9 horizontals** for full-bleed
  breaks and hero video stills. Leave intentional **negative space** in some
  wides for headline placement.
- **Include genuine second parties** (a real client/collaborator, hands only is
  fine) so "professional interaction" doesn't look staged.

## 4 · Shared — Meetings & Professional Interaction
- Client meeting across a table, both engaged (wide + medium + hands detail).
- Laura listening (not talking) — trust reads in the listening frame.
- Handshake-free greeting / walking-into-a-room moment (avoid cliché).
- Presenting to a small group, editorial and calm.

## 5 · Shared — Office / Environment
- Wide establishing shot of the workspace, empty of people (context/atmosphere).
- Laura walking through the space (motion, candid).
- Desk vignette: books, printed matter, glasses, a single object of taste.
- Window light across a neutral wall (texture plate for backgrounds).

## 6 · Shared — Detail & Editorial Lifestyle
- Hands writing in a notebook.
- Turning pages of a magazine / book.
- Coffee set down mid-work (no branding, no cliché staging).
- Paper, type specimens, fabric, material close-ups (atmosphere between sections).
- One or two black-and-white frames for reflective/personal moments only.

- **Featured Work / case studies** gain real texture (still needs actual client

  studio portraits.

  breathes and stops reading as a single shoot.
- **Hero video** stills and the compressed loops can be captured in the same session.

## Deliverable spec
Minimum ~8–12 usable frames per visual world; RAW + graded exports; verticals
prioritized (3:4 / 4:5), a handful of 16:9; consistent warm-neutral grade; no
heavy retouching (skin stays real); provide 2–3 frames per world with clean
negative space for typography.
